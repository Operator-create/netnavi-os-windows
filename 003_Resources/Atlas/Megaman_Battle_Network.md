# Megaman Battle Network 💻⚡

The structural inspiration for this OS. In the *Megaman Battle Network* universe, human Operators carry Personal Terminals (PETs) that house intelligent digital avatars called NetNavis.

- **The Operator:** You. The human providing governance, approval, and creative direction.
- **The NetNavi:** The AI agent (like Antigravity) that executes commands, parses data, and manages the digital environment.
- **Jack In:** The act of initiating a terminal bridge (like the Gemini CLI Action Layer) to interact with the broader internet or local system.
