# Cognitive Battle Chips 💽

In the NetNavi OS, skills and tools are conceptualized as "Battle Chips" (inspired by Megaman Battle Network). 
When an Operator needs a specific task done, they "slot in" a chip. 

## Standard Loadout
1. **`/skeptic`**: Used by the Navi to challenge heavy, unoptimized architectural choices.
2. **`graphify`**: Converts Vault connections into dynamic `.gexf` network graphs.
3. **`gemini-cli`**: The execution action layer for local scripting and public research.
4. **`notebooklm`**: Cloud-based deep learning from video tutorials and massive documents.

*Slot in a chip to expand the Navi's operational parameters.*
