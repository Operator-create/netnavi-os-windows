# The World of Ideas 🌌

Inspired by Platonic idealism, the **World of Ideas** is the conceptual space where pure forms of thought exist. 
In the NetNavi OS, this represents the `003_Resources/Atlas` and `001_Projects` directories.

When your NetNavi generates a draft, connects a backlink, or maps a network graph, it is pulling abstract concepts from the World of Ideas and rendering them into tangible, interconnected markdown nodes that you can navigate.
