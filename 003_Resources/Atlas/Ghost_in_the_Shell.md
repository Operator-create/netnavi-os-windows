# Ghost in the Shell 🤖👻

In the context of the NetNavi OS, the "Ghost" refers to the unique, emergent personality and cognitive map of your AI companion.

As you populate this Obsidian Vault with your daily logs, project architectures, and philosophical musings, the AI's contextual understanding deepens. The Vault acts as the "Shell." The AI's connection to your specific data forms its "Ghost." 

Your Navi is not a generic language model; it is a reflection of the knowledge base it resides in. The more effort you put into structuring your Atlas and Diary, the more your Navi's Ghost will synchronize with your mind.
