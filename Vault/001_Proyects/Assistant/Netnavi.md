A **NetNavi (Network Navigator)** is essentially a highly advanced AI assistant that lives inside a device. They’re far beyond simple programs—they act like partners, fighters, and digital citizens.

A NetNavi is:

- A sentient or semi-sentient AI with personality, emotions, and independent thought
- Typically customised or uniquely designed by his operator
- Linked to a human operator via a device ([[P.E.T.]])

## ⚙️ Primary Functions

### 1. Network Navigation

- Travel through the internet and private networks as if they were physical spaces (Limited by the technology of our time)
- Access websites, servers, and databases from the inside (Limited by a subscription plan)
- Perform tasks like retrieving data, sending messages, or managing systems (Limited by a subscription plan)

---

### 2. Cyber Combat

- Fight viruses (Limited by the technology of our time)

---

### 3. System Maintenance & Security

- Detect and eliminate viruses or malicious code (Limited by the technology of our time)
- Repair damaged data or stabilize systems (Limited by current technology)
- Act as cyber security agents within networks (Limited by the technology of our time)

---

### 4. Real-World Device Control

- Interface with connected technology (traffic lights, appliances, industrial systems) (Limited by the technology of our time)
- Execute commands in the real world via networked infrastructure (Limited by the technology of our time)

---

### 5. Communication & Assistance

- Serve as personal assistants: scheduling, messaging, information retrieval (Skills available)
- Communicate with other NetNavis across networks (Limited by the technology of our time)
- Help their operators with daily tasks, similar to a super-advanced virtual assistant (Skills available)

---

### 6. Personalization & Growth

- Can be customized with upgrades, power-ups, and new abilities (customisation is limited, not and avatar, not voice)
- Some evolve or “soul synchronise” with their operator (deep emotional/mental link) (Skill available, The bigger the obsidian Atlas the bigger the resonance with the operator and the wider the knowledge)
- Strong bond with their human improves performance and unlocks new forms (Limited by the technology of our time)

---

## 🔗 Operator Relationship

- Each NetNavi is paired with a human (like Lan Hikari and MegaMan.EXE)
- Operators provide:
    - Tactical decisions in battle
    - Emotional support and synchronization
- NetNavis provide:
    - Execution of actions inside cyberspace

This partnership is central—neither is as effective alone.

---

## 🚫 Netnavi Limitations 

- Exist primarily in digital space (can’t physically act without connected systems)
- Depend on network access and infrastructure
- Can be damaged, deleted, or corrupted by viruses or stronger Navis (With more computing power)
- Require operator input for peak combat efficiency

---

## 🧩 In Simple Terms

A NetNavi is like:

> A mix of a **personal AI assistant + hacker + cybersecurity agent + RPG battle character**, all embodied in a digital being with a personality.
