It is the central piece of technology that dictates daily life, infrastructure, and communication in a completely digitised society in [[Megaman Battle Network]]

### 📱The P.E.T. (Personal Terminal): 
is far more than just a sleek handheld menu for the player; Stripping away its function as a video game user interface, a P.E.T. serves several critical lore and practical functions

#### In-game functions:
 1. "Housing" and Managing the [[Netnavi]] personalization and state: The primary function of a P.E.T. is to act as the physical home and hardware host for a NetNavi (Network Assistant). Life Support & Storage and backup: The P.E.T. contains the memory, personality data, and core programming of a Navi. 
 2. Daily All-in-One Personal Device For the average citizen who isn't a dedicated NetBattler, the P.E.T. is the ultimate smartphone, PC, and wallet replacement. Its everyday functions include: Communication: Standard video calling, text messaging, and emails. Digital Wallet: Handling financial transactions, ID verification, and electronic shopping. Information Terminal: Accessing the real-world internet, checking maps, and reading the news. 
 3. Navi Customization: It acts as the hardware interface to install upgrades, such as the Navi Customizer program, to alter a Navi's statistics, abilities, and code structure. 
 4. Network Interface and "Jack-In" Capabilities The world of Battle Network relies on everything being computerized, from refrigerators and traffic lights to massive server banks. The Universal Key: A P.E.T. features a built-in cable (or wireless connection in later models) allowing the Operator to "Jack-In" their NetNavi into almost any electronic device. Cyberworld Exploration: Once jacked in, the P.E.T. transmits the Navi into that device’s specific "Cyberworld" to fix glitches, fight viruses, or operate the machinery. 
 5. Battle Chip Downloading and Operator Support During NetBattles or virus eradication, the P.E.T. is the physical bridge that allows a human operator to support their Navi in real-time. Chip Slot-In: Operators physically slide Battle Chips into the P.E.T.’s drive. The P.E.T. translates the physical chip data into digital weapon data, downloading it instantly to the Navi on the Net. Tactical Analysis: The P.E.T. analyzes the battlefield, monitors the Navi's remaining health (HP), and alerts the operator to virus weaknesses and incoming threats.
 6. Physical Security and Emergency: Because the physical world and the digital world are so deeply intertwined in the world of [[Megaman Battle Network]], P.E.Ts are equipped with tools to handle real-world emergencies resulting from Net-crimes. Pulse Transmission (Later Models): Advanced P.E.Ts can convert a human operator's mind into data to "Wave-In" or "Pulse-In" to the cyberworld directly. 
 7. Physical Scanning: They can scan physical objects (like barcodes or ID cards) to convert real-world data into digital items (connections) or keys for the NetNavi to use.
